Lost and Found

Welcome to Lost and Found

A school platform where students and staff can report, find, and claim lost belongings.

HOW IT WORKS?

01 — Report an Item

Students or staff submit a report for a lost or found belonging, including its name, category, description, date, and location.

02 — Search for Belongings

Browse reported items and use search or category filters to find a possible match.

03 — Submit a Claim

If an item belongs to you, submit a claim and provide details that can help verify ownership.

04 — Verify and Retrieve

Wait for the authorized staff member or administrator to review the claim. Once approved, follow the school's instructions to retrieve your item.

OUR MISSION & VISION

Our Mission

To provide a reliable and accessible platform where students and school personnel can report, locate, and reclaim lost belongings through responsible reporting, proper verification, and cooperation. We aim to promote honesty, accountability, and care within the school community.

Our Vision

To become a trusted school Lost and Found platform that fosters a community where every lost belonging has a better chance of being returned to its rightful owner, and where honesty, trust, and responsibility are practiced by every member of the school community.
